# Claim registry — UNIT (the non-hero actor: its template, its instance, its combat inputs)

Level 2 ledger. Index: [registry.md](registry.md) · spec: [`formats/unit/format.md`](../formats/unit/format.md). Legend: `✔ promoted` (also in a spec) · `● active` · `✖ retracted`. IDs are permanent.

Not a file format. This ledger owns the **second arm** of actor creation: everything between a
`Data.bin` **Units** row and a live monster that can hit something.
[`claims/hero.md`](hero.md) owns the *human* arm — chargen, the derived-stat graph and the
combat loop's resolver — and the two arms share exactly one routine, the damage resolver
`FUN_004fbc92` (`HERO-DAMAGE-022`), which is why the split is by vtable rather than by topic:
`vt+0x50` is `FUN_004f7dfc` on the two human classes and `FUN_004f5946` on the base class every
non-hero actor uses, and the second body derives nothing. [`claims/databin.md`](databin.md) owns
the file the template lives in; [`claims/anim.md`](anim.md) owns the client drawable's *animation*. The two `UNIT-PANEL-*`
rows below sit on the boundary — they are about which of this ledger's values cross to the
drawable, so they are filed here rather than there.
