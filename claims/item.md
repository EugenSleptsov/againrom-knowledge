# Claim registry — ITEM (the item, its container and the sack)

Level 2 ledger. Index: [registry.md](registry.md). Legend: `✔ promoted` (also in a spec) ·
`● active` · `✖ retracted`. IDs are permanent.

Not a file format. This is the simulation's **carrying** layer: what an item is as an object,
the container a unit and a sack both hold one of, and the commands that move an item between
them. It sits on `world.res:data/data.bin` (`claims/databin.md`) for definitions, on
`claims/sav.md` for the stream, and beside `claims/shop.md`, which owns the *generation* and
*pricing* of the same objects and already published one of these commands (`SHOP-TRAY-025`).

**Do not confuse this ledger with [`inv.md`](inv.md)** — that one is the *install corpus and
signatures* ledger (`INV-CORPUS-001`, `INV-SIG-002`, …), about files on disk. It has nothing
to do with a game inventory and no game-item row belongs in it.

Vocabulary below is the engine's own. An **item** is any instance of the four `Item`-rooted
classes. The **container** is the unnamed `CObList` subclass a unit holds at `actor+0x7c` and
a sack at `sack+0x40`; the ledger calls its `+0x20` field the **load**. A **sack** is the
`Sack` class. "Equipped" always means one of the fourteen pointer fields of `ITEM-EQUIP-006`,
never a member of the container.
