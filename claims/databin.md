Level 2 ledger. Index: [registry.md](registry.md) · spec: [`formats/databin/format.md`](../formats/databin/format.md). Legend: `✔ promoted` (also in the spec) · `● active` · `✖ retracted`. IDs are permanent.

parameters (`ALM-CLS-036`, `ALM-CLS-038`) — the "what file fills it" question EXP-0037
left open, answered by [EXP-0049](../experiments/EXP-0049-placeable-db/).
