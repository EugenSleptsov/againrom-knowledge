# Claim registry — TAVERN (mercenary hire)

Level 2 ledger. Index: [registry.md](registry.md). Legend: `✔ promoted` (also in a spec) ·
`● active` · `✖ retracted`. IDs are permanent.

Not a file format — the **town building** whose other half `REG-SCN-064` already covers. The inn
is two screens' worth of machinery over one view: a *mission-giver* (`InnMission` / `InnNPC`,
EXP-0060) and a *hiring hall* (this ledger). They share the view, the record and nothing else.

Vocabulary below is the engine's own indexing, not ours. A **type** is the 1-based subscript
`byte [unit + 0x15b]` (client) / `byte [unit + 0x14c]` (server) carries; it is also the
`[npc<t>]` section number in `scenario.res::npc.reg` and the index into `[General]
MercenaryCount`. There are fifteen of them. A **pool** is how many units of one type exist; a
**shelf** is what a given mission offers.
